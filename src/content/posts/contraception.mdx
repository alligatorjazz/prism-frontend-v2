## What is Contraception?

Contraception is the use of artificial methods to prevent pregnancy when engaging in sexual intercourse.

## Type of Contraception

### Contraception can be broken into five main types:

- [Condoms](https://www.prismfl.org/post/barrier-methods)
- [Diaphragms](https://www.prismfl.org/post/diaphragms)
- [Cervical Caps](https://www.prismfl.org/post/cervical-caps)
- [Birth Control](https://www.prismfl.org/post/birth-control)
- [Intrauterine Devices (IUDs)](https://www.prismfl.org/post/_iuds)
- [Implants](https://www.prismfl.org/post/implants)
- [Sterilization](www.prismfl.org/post/sterilization)

You can click on any of these to find out more information.

## The Importance of Contraception

### Contraception can prevent unwanted pregnancies.

Using contraceptive methods allows you and anyone you have sex with to determine when you have children, how many you want to have, and when to stop having children.

### Barrier methods protect you from STIs.

Barrier methods, such as [**condoms**](https://www.prismfl.org/post/barrier-methods) and [**dental dams**](https://www.prismfl.org/post/dental-dams), are extremely effective in protecting you from a host of sexually transmitted infections.
